Design a premium modern fintech landing page for “Money Jar” focused on International Transfers and FX Services.

The design must feel:

* calm
* trustworthy
* simple
* premium
* human
* globally connected
* mobile-first
* emotionally reassuring rather than corporate banking

Avoid:

* aggressive fintech aesthetics
* crypto styling
* trading platform visuals
* overly technical finance UI
* dark mode neon aesthetics
* cluttered comparison tables

The page should follow best-practice fintech UX patterns inspired by Wise, Revolut, Monzo and modern European neobank interfaces, while maintaining a softer and more approachable personality.

GOAL:
Create a conversion-focused landing page that helps users:

* understand international transfer services quickly
* trust the pricing transparency
* see transfer simplicity
* understand transfer timing
* open an account

The page must comply visually and structurally with:

* WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility standards
* EU accessibility best practices
* proper semantic hierarchy
* high contrast ratios
* keyboard navigation logic
* large tap targets for mobile
* readable typography scaling
* accessible form field spacing
* screen-reader-friendly content grouping
